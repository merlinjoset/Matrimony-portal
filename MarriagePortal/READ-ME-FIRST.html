What to try

  • Switch language - use the EN / தமிழ் toggle (top-right). The whole site changes instantly.
  • Browse & filter profiles by congregation, denomination and bride/groom.
  • Register a profile - add one or more photos, fill the form, and submit. Your new profile appears in Browse with its photo.
  • Approve it - open the Admin Portal → Verification Queue; the profile you just submitted appears there with its photo. Click Approve. end-to-end demo
  • Explore the admin Dashboard, Members, Interests, Parishes, Users & Roles.

What's included in this prototype

  • Faith-centred matrimony service for the CSI Tamil parish community
  • Full English & Tamil bilingual interface
  • Profile photo upload (up to 5 photos, with a main photo)
  • Parish verification workflow (profiles approved before going live)
  • Privacy: contact details shared only after mutual interest
  • Admin: members, interests, congregations, reports, and staff users & roles
  • Real parish identity - CSI emblem, Dubai contact details, the 3 UAE congregations
Please note: This is a visual & functional prototype for approval. Sample profiles are illustrative. Actions such as Add user, Export and Send interest show confirmation messages but are not yet connected to a live database - that comes in the build phase once the design is approved.

How to open

Double-click READ-ME-FIRST.html (this page) and use the buttons above, or open index.html directly. Works best in Chrome, Edge or Firefox. For the full end-to-end demo, keep both the public site and admin portal open in the same browser.
